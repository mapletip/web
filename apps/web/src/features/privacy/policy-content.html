Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

MapleTip is a free website providing MapleStory game information, tools, and resources. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little personal information as necessary to operate the website.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you visit https://mapletip.com.

This policy applies only to mapletip.com. It does not apply to MapleWiki (wiki.mapletip.com), which maintains a separate privacy policy at MapleWiki's Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit MapleTip, our hosting, advertising, and infrastructure providers may automatically process technical information, including:

  • Your IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Pages requested and the date and time of access
  • Referring website addresses
  • Technical information related to website performance, security, and advertising

MapleTip does not currently offer user accounts, registration, or public content submission features.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to provide.

2. How We Use Information

Information associated with visits to MapleTip may be used to:

  • Deliver game information and website functionality
  • Maintain website performance and reliability
  • Detect and prevent malicious activity and abuse
  • Understand aggregate website traffic and usage
  • Display non-personalized advertisements
  • Respond to inquiries and privacy requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Where applicable, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating and securing MapleTip, compliance with legal obligations, and consent when required for advertising cookies or similar technologies.

We do not create individual advertising profiles or maintain lists of visitors for behavioral advertising.

3. Advertising and Cookies

MapleTip uses Google AdSense to help support the operation of this free website.

MapleTip requests non-personalized advertisements. These advertisements are selected using contextual information, such as the content of the page you are viewing or your approximate location, rather than your past browsing behavior or advertising interests.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve advertisements. Across their advertising services, these technologies may enable advertising based on visits to this website or other websites. MapleTip does not request personalized advertising.

Even with non-personalized advertisements, Google and participating advertising vendors may process information through cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and similar technologies for purposes including:

  • Delivering advertisements
  • Limiting how frequently advertisements appear
  • Aggregated advertising reporting and measurement
  • Detecting advertising fraud and abuse

Third-party vendors may place or read cookies on your browser or use similar technologies to collect information when advertisements are served.

You can learn more about Google's data practices and manage your advertising choices:

  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings
  • YourAdChoices

If MapleTip adds other participating advertising vendors, we will identify them in this policy and provide their applicable privacy and opt-out information.

4. Your Cookie and Privacy Choices

MapleTip and its infrastructure provider may use cookies and similar technologies necessary for website operation and security.

Cloudflare may use cookies to identify malicious traffic, prevent abuse, and protect legitimate visitors.

MapleTip may store interface information in your browser. The website currently uses session storage to remember when a historical-version notice has been dismissed. It does not currently store appearance or display preferences.

You can manage or delete cookies and website storage through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality or security checks.

Where required by applicable law, MapleTip requests consent before using advertising cookies or similar technologies. You may manage or withdraw consent using privacy controls when provided on the website.

We do not independently respond to legacy browser Do Not Track signals. Applicable legally recognized privacy preference signals, including Global Privacy Control, are handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Disabling advertising cookies does not prevent you from accessing MapleTip's game information, although it may affect the advertisements displayed.

6. Information Sharing

We share technical information with our hosting, infrastructure, and advertising providers as necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and support MapleTip.

We may also preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal requests, investigate abuse, or protect the security of the website.

We do not sell visitor contact lists or intentionally use visitors' browsing histories for personalized advertising.

Certain disclosures to advertising providers may qualify as a sale, sharing, or targeted advertising under applicable privacy laws. Where required, we provide appropriate privacy choices.

7. Data Retention and Security

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Technical information may be retained by our hosting, security, and advertising providers according to their applicable retention practices.

Information you send directly to us is retained as necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain appropriate records, and meet legal obligations.

We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information about you
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent
  • Request portability of personal information where applicable
  • Opt out of certain advertising-related data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with an appropriate data protection authority

You may submit privacy requests using the contact information below. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

Some information processed independently by third-party providers may be subject to their own privacy request procedures.

9. Children's Privacy

MapleTip is a general-audience game reference website and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

We do not currently offer accounts or features designed to solicit personal information from children.

If you believe personal information about a child has been improperly collected or disclosed, please contact us so we can review the matter.

Information associated with website visits may be processed in the United States or other countries by our hosting, infrastructure, and advertising providers, subject to applicable legal requirements.
